About Our Circle

We are the Class of 2024 - a group of dreamers, achievers, and lifelong friends.

Our journey began in the corridors of our beloved college, where strangers became classmates, and classmates became family. Through late-night study sessions, exam stress, college fests, and countless memories, we built bonds that will last a lifetime.

This platform is our digital home - a place to stay connected, share updates, plan reunions, and keep our friendship alive no matter where life takes us.
